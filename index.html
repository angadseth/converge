Three courses, three different skills

MLF is maths. MLT is algorithms. MLP is code.

MLF

Calculus, linear algebra, optimisation and probability. Nearly every question is a calculation by hand, so practise problems every single week.

MLT

PCA to SVMs to neural networks. Know what each model assumes, what it minimises and how it trains. Derive it; don't memorise it.

MLP

The same models in scikit-learn, marked on code: two OPPEs and three Kaggle assignments. Your numbers have to match exactly.
